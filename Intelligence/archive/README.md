**Summary**: Finished projects, moved here whole from `Projects/`. Empty until your first project finishes.

A project arrives with its full folder, its README marked `status: archived`, and a one-line callout at the top stating when and why it closed. Every link that pointed at its old location was repointed in the same pass that moved it.

Nothing here is edited afterwards. The archive is the permanent record; if an archived project restarts, it moves back to `Projects/` as a new pass, and the archive keeps the record of the first run.
