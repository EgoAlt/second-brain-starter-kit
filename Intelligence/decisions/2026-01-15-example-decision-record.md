**Summary**: Demo decision record: the sample project uses carbon spars rather than fibreglass for its first frame. Invented content showing the record shape; delete with the rest of the demo.

## Decision

Build the first frame from carbon tube.

## Options considered

1. **Carbon tube.** Stiff, light, springs back from crash loads. Roughly twice the cost of fibreglass.
2. **Fibreglass rod.** Cheap and forgiving of a first build, but heavy enough that the kite would need more wind than the target range.

## Reasoning

The project's stated goal is to learn the craft, and the wind range at the intended flying field is low. A frame that needs more wind would mean fewer flights, which is the one thing a learning project cannot afford. Cost was explicitly not the constraint. Detail on the frame is in [[Resources/library/sample-topic/kite-building|kite-building]].

## Revisit if

The first two frames break within a month. At that point fibreglass for the practice frame becomes the better trade.
