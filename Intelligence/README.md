# Intelligence

Records that are true at a point in time and stay that way: decisions and why they were made, meeting notes, second opinions, and the archive of finished projects. Unlike a project README, which is rewritten to stay current, files here are append-only history.

| Subfolder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `decisions/` | One record per decision, dated, with the options considered and the reason for the choice. Also [[decisions/rule-provenance|rule-provenance.md]], the origin story of every numbered rule. |
| [[archive/README|archive/]] | Finished projects, moved here whole from `Projects/` with their inbound links repointed. |

Add `meetings/`, `transcripts/`, or `market/` when you have content for them.
