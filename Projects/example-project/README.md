## Overview

Build a two-line delta stunt kite with a 1.8 m wingspan from carbon spars and ripstop. The point is to learn the craft, not to save money: a comparable kite costs less than the tools. Reference material lives in [[Resources/library/sample-topic/kite-building|kite-building]] and [[Resources/library/sample-topic/bridle-tuning|bridle-tuning]].

## Current Status

Frame cut and joined. Sail pattern traced but not yet sewn. Bridle points marked on the spine at the positions from the tuning guide, pending a first flight to adjust. No flight test yet.

## Next Steps

1. Sew the sail panels and reinforce the nose.
2. Fit the bridle at the marked points, then check the tow-point angle against the table in [[Resources/library/sample-topic/bridle-tuning|bridle-tuning]].
3. First flight in 8 to 15 km/h wind. Log the result here and adjust the bridle.
